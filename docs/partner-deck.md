# Partner deck: content for Claude Design

Ten slides for a business in Egypt that could host our recording teams, or run its own team on our phones. Content only. Formatting is for Claude Design. Every line follows the company map's rules: plain English, no client name, no app name, the work is described as "an AI project". Money terms are left for the Commercial Director to fill in before the meeting.

Audience: the owner or general manager of a factory, warehouse, farm, hotel, or construction site in Egypt, and delivery partners who run their own teams.

Tone: calm, direct, factual. Short sentences. We are a serious operation with rules, and that is the point.

## Slide 2. What we do

We record video of real work, from the worker's point of view, for an AI project.

- A worker wears a small camera on a cap and does their normal job.
- Nothing about the job changes. No script, no acting, no extra tasks.
- The footage goes to an AI project. We review it ourselves before it goes anywhere.

One line to remember: the work you already do is worth money, and nothing about it has to change.

Speaker note: if asked what the AI project is, the answer is "an AI project, under a confidentiality agreement". Nothing more. Never name a client, a product, or what the footage is for.

---

## Slide 3. What it looks like on your floor

- A recording shift is 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM, six days a week, on the days you choose.
- Each worker wears a cap with a phone mount. One of our operators looks after every ten phones.
- The phone records in 30-minute cycles. The operator restarts it and checks the angle.
- Phones charge at a station we set up. At night the footage uploads, either on your internet or through our hub.
- Our people are quiet, trained, and follow your site's safety rules and PPE.

Speaker note: bring a cap and a phone to the meeting. Let them hold it.

## Slide 5. Two ways to work with us

**Host site.** You provide the floor and the workers. We bring the phones, the operators, and the management. You are paid for accepted hours.

**Delivery partner.** You hire and run your own team on our phones, following our standards. You deliver finished hours to us and are paid for what is accepted.

Both follow the same rules. A partner's hours are as good as our own, because they pass the same checks.

---

## Slide 6. Our rules, and why they protect you

- **Every worker is screened first.** ID copy, age 18 or older, a signed consent form, and a fraud briefing. Nobody records without it.
- **We review every video before it goes anywhere.** Our own reviewers watch it the next day.
- **Only real work counts.** Footage of nothing, wrong angles, or staged tasks is removed and not paid.
- **Privacy is respected.** We never record in changing rooms, bathrooms, prayer rooms, or anywhere people expect privacy. Signs go up where we record.
- **Your site's rules come first.** Safety, access hours, and restricted areas are agreed before launch day and written down.

Speaker note: the rules are what make the hours worth paying for. A serious buyer needs a serious operation.

---

## Slide 7. The phones stay ours, and stay safe

- Every phone is managed remotely: locked to the recording app, tracked on a map, and wiped if lost.
- Phones stay on your site, in our hub, or in a sealed bag someone signed for. Never in anyone's home.
- Both sides sign at every handover. A weekly count keeps the numbers matching.
- If a phone goes missing, we handle it. Your team is not chased for it.

## Slide 8. From first visit to first payment

1. **Visit.** Our Commercial Director walks your site with you: where we record, where we never record, power, storage, internet.
2. **Agree.** Terms approved, agreement signed, consent forms explained.
3. **Launch day.** We set up the charging station, label every phone, test the network, and train each worker for ten minutes.
4. **Record.** Daily shifts. A site lead on your floor sends a daily report by 6:00 PM.
5. **Review.** Every video is checked the next day.
6. **Pay.** Monthly statement on accepted hours, paid on time.
